# Future Work

This dataset could be expanded computationally in several ways.

One possible next step would involve using APIs such as Semantic Scholar or OpenAlex to automatically collect larger numbers of scholarly articles related to AI in education. Natural language processing methods could also be used to identify recurring themes, keywords, or shifts in educational discourse over time.

Another possible expansion would involve comparing scholarly discussions with public discussions on social media platforms, educational blogs, or teacher forums. This could help identify differences between academic perspectives and public perceptions of AI in education.

The dataset could also be expanded geographically by including more non-English educational sources and research from underrepresented regions.

Finally, future work could involve more detailed analysis of how generative AI tools such as ChatGPT are reshaping educational policies, classroom practices, and perceptions of student productivity.
