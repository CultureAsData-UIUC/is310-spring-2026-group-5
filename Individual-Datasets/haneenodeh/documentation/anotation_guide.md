# Annotation Guide

This dataset tracks how scholarly sources discuss AI use in K-12 education, especially middle school and high school contexts.

Each row represents one scholarly article or academic source.

## Categories

### Education Level
- middle school: focuses specifically on middle school students
- high school: focuses specifically on high school students
- K-12: discusses school education broadly
- unclear: education level is not clearly stated

### AI Tool
- ChatGPT: specifically names ChatGPT
- generative AI: discusses generative AI broadly
- AI tutoring system: AI used for tutoring or personalized support
- automated grading: AI used for assessment or feedback
- other: another AI tool or system
- unclear: tool is not clearly identified

### Main Theme
- productivity
- writing assistance
- academic integrity
- personalized learning
- teacher attitudes
- student attitudes
- policy
- accessibility
- ethics
- unclear

### Overall Tone
- positive: mostly emphasizes benefits
- negative: mostly emphasizes risks or concerns
- mixed: includes both benefits and concerns
- neutral: descriptive without strong judgment

## Notes
The labels are interpretive. When an article fits more than one category, I choose the most central theme as the main_theme and place the second most important theme under secondary_theme.
