# AI in K-12 Education Dataset

## Project Overview

This project examines how scholarly and academic sources discuss artificial intelligence in K-12 education between approximately 2020 and 2026. Rather than attempting to directly measure student productivity, this dataset focuses on how researchers, educators, and institutions describe the opportunities, concerns, and educational implications of AI technologies in school settings.

The dataset explores themes such as:
- teacher attitudes toward AI
- student engagement
- academic integrity
- AI literacy
- personalized learning
- educational productivity
- ethics and policy

This project fits within the broader group theme:
**“AI in Academic, Online, and Social Spaces.”**

---

# Dataset Structure

Each row in the dataset represents one scholarly source.

The dataset includes:
- scholarly journal articles
- conference papers
- reports
- academic book chapters
- educational technology publications

The dataset was manually annotated using interpretive categories including:
- education level
- AI tool type
- main theme
- secondary theme
- overall tone
- benefits discussed
- concerns discussed

---

# Limitations

This dataset does not directly measure actual student productivity or educational outcomes. Instead, it captures how scholarly literature discusses AI in educational contexts.

Some article metadata was incomplete or inconsistent across sources. In some cases, categorization decisions were based primarily on abstracts or article summaries rather than full-text review.

---

# Ethical Considerations

The dataset does not contain personal student data, survey responses, or classroom records. All sources were publicly accessible scholarly or academic publications.

However, the dataset still reflects potential biases within published academic literature, including uneven geographic representation and differing institutional perspectives on AI adoption.
